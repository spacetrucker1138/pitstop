Quick party food & drinks

Twelve ideas, mostly ten minutes or less, mostly stuff you already have or can grab fast.

  • Snack

    Mummy hot dogs

    Wrap hot dogs in thin strips of crescent dough, bake until golden, add mustard dots for eyes.

  • Snack

    Banana ghosts

    Halve bananas, stand them up, press on mini chocolate chips for eyes and mouth.

  • Snack

    Clementine pumpkins

    Peel a clementine, stick a small piece of celery in the top as the stem. Done.

  • Snack

    Spider crackers

    Sandwich peanut butter (or cream cheese, or a nut-free spread) between two round crackers, add pretzel stick legs.

  • Snack

    Monster popcorn

    Pop a big bowl, toss with melted chocolate or butter, then add candy corn and any leftover candy.

  • Snack

    Witch fingers

    Pretzel rods dipped in melted green candy melts or white chocolate, with a sliced almond for the nail.

  • Snack

    Graveyard dip

    Bean dip or hummus in a dish, crumbled chips or crushed cookies as dirt, and cracker or cookie tombstones on top.

  • Snack

    Monster-face quesadillas

    Cut faces out of a tortilla, lay it over a whole one with cheese, and toast in a pan or oven.

  • Drink

    Witch's brew punch

    Lime sherbet or green sports drink with ginger ale. Add gummy worms around the rim.

  • Drink

    Blood-red fizz

    Cranberry or cherry juice over ice, topped with lemon-lime soda. A squeeze of lime keeps it bright.

  • Drink

    Dirt cup hot chocolate

    Hot cocoa topped with crushed chocolate cookies and a gummy worm on the mug rim.

  • Drink

    Orange soda cauldron float

    Orange soda over vanilla ice cream. Looks like a bubbling cauldron, takes 30 seconds.

Check ingredients for allergies before you serve anything to guests.
