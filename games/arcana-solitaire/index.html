How to Play

Goal: build all four suit altars from Ace up to King: Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles.

Columns: stack cards in descending order (King, Queen, Knight, Page, 10…2, Ace). Each card must be a different suit from the card it lands on. Only a King can fill an empty column.

Deck: tap the deck to turn cards (1 or 3 at a time, see Settings). When it runs out, tap the empty spot to gather the cards again.

Quick moves: tap a card to send it to its best spot. Drag to choose. Press and hold any card to see it full size.

Keys: Z undo · H hint · Space draw
