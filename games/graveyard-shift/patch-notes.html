🐗 GRAVE HOG’S GRAVEYARD SHIFT: PATCH NOTES

Oct 25, 2026

NEW: THE LOOT MACHINE

Chests got a glow-up. Crack one open and the shift pauses while Grave Hog dives in headfirst, digs through the junk (bottles, bones, a lollipop, somebody’s skull necklace…) and comes up with the goods.

  • Every chest now gives +1 level to a weapon or perk you already own
  • All maxed out? You get a full heal + 25 wages instead
  • Tap, click or press Enter to skip the animation
  • Replaces the old +2% treasure bonuses, which weren’t worth the walk

ZOOM

  • Zoom now works on phones and tablets: pinch with two fingers or tap the new ZOOM button
  • On PC: mouse wheel, Z to cycle, or – / =
  • On a controller: the bumpers
  • You can now zoom out farther (up to 2×)
  • Your zoom level is remembered between stages and sessions

PIZZA & THE MAINTENANCE OFFICE

  • Pizza now heals a flat 5 HP (fixed a bug where it sometimes healed only 2 while claiming +20)
  • NEW office upgrade: Extra Cheese, +3 HP per pizza per level (up to 5 levels)
  • Visiting the office from the Shift Complete screen no longer ends your run. Back returns you to the results so you can continue
  • The Stage 1 results screen now says CONTINUE TO STAGE 2, because yes, there’s a second shift

KEEPING THE ACTION ON SCREEN

  • Enemies can no longer snipe you from off-screen. Mega Mart throwers, drones and forklifts (and the Widow and the King) only attack when you can see them
  • Your weapons now focus on monsters on screen, so no more Deadanomicon black holes opening somewhere you can’t see

BALANCE

  • Stage 1 fights back: monsters get much tougher as the night goes on. No more standing still to win
  • Nail Gun reworked: +1 nail per level (up to 5), each nail a little weaker. Still strong, no longer absurd at level 5
  • Forklifts: no more than 2 on the floor at once
  • All bosses: +5% health
  • The Cemetery King: his skulls track you harder and longer, and he aims later in his cast. Sidestepping early won’t cut it anymore
  • The Cart Beast: 10% bigger. It was looking a little underfed
  • Funeral Bell: no more knockback. Each toll now sends monsters fleeing in terror for 1 second per bell level. Scared monsters can’t attack or hurt you. Bosses are too stubborn to scare

POLISH

  • The Grave Digger and Widow stay down a couple of seconds longer before fading. Enjoy the moment
  • The Nail Gun sound now varies in pitch, so rapid fire sounds like a nail gun and not a metronome
  • Long alert messages now wrap instead of running off the screen
  • One-time tip on your first shift showing how to zoom

BUG FIXES

  • Fixed the game freezing when opening a chest while playing the downloaded version from a folder
  • Fixed pizza sometimes healing for less than it said
