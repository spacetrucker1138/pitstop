Welcome to DEAD AIR, the deadliest game show in the galaxy! The members of JURN have been kidnapped by the sinister V1SION corporation and thrown into a televised nightmare where survival means ratings, and death means GREAT ratings!

Blast your way through industrial arenas crawling with enemies, grab outrageous power-ups, and face monstrous bosses while Trash Queen, your unwilling host, tries to keep the audience entertained... and occasionally keep you alive.

Inspired by the chaotic, quarter-munching arcade shooters of the '90s, JURN: Dead Air delivers relentless twin-stick action, dark humor, heavy metal attitude, and enough explosions to make the network executives nervous.

Four rockers. One twisted game show. Zero commercial breaks.
